How motion is resolved

Every frame, each joint takes its value from the highest active layer: rest pose and breathing, then animation clips, commanded actions, gaze (commands, then tracking), facial expression and blinks, speech, and finally manual sliders. A safety layer then clamps every joint to its limit and caps its speed. Commands from Claude or the local parser pass through a validator first; unknown actions are rejected and out-of-range values are clamped.
